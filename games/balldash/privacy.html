2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and how the Game is configured, we may collect the following categories of information:

2.1. Account and Profile Data
We may collect identifiers and profile-related information made available through platform services, such as Google Play Games player ID, display name, and related basic profile information.

2.2. Gameplay and Progression Data
We may collect gameplay-related information such as progress, levels, inventory, virtual currencies, rewards, missions, settings, achievements, save-state information, and session events.

2.3. Multiplayer, Marketplace, and Trading Data
We may collect room or session identifiers, matchmaking status, transaction or offer information, trade actions, trade status, marketplace listings, and related integrity or fraud signals.

2.4. Chat and Community Data
Where chat or related communication features are enabled, we may collect message content, timestamps, sender and recipient identifiers, moderation flags, reports, and records of enforcement actions or investigations.

2.5. Device and Technical Data
We may collect technical and diagnostic information such as app version, device model, operating system version, language, region, time zone, IP address or network metadata, crash data, logs, diagnostics, performance data, and similar technical information.

2.6. Purchase and Subscription Data
Where in-app purchases or subscriptions are offered, we may collect product identifiers, purchase state, subscription state, entitlement status, and purchase tokens or related transaction metadata provided through Google Play Billing or other authorized billing systems. We do not receive your full payment card or bank account details from Google Play.

2.7. Advertising Data
Where advertising is enabled, we may collect or receive ad-related data, including ad interactions, advertising identifiers, impression and engagement data, and related attribution information, subject to your device settings, platform controls, consent status where applicable, and applicable law.

2.8. Notification Data
Where notifications are used, we may collect notification preferences, delivery status, and engagement events associated with push or local notifications.
