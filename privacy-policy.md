# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 14, 2026**

This app scans nearby Bluetooth and Wi-Fi signals to detect supported smartglasses and possible media-transfer activity. Signal information is processed on the device and is not sent to us or shared with third parties.

Android may require Bluetooth, Nearby Wi-Fi, location, notification, and foreground-service permissions. Location permission is used only because Android requires it for wireless scanning; this app does not collect or store your GPS location.

App settings are stored locally. Detection history is kept only for the active session. A diagnostic log is created only when you choose to export one. This app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

For questions, visit the [project repository](https://github.com/AkshitSharma1/smartglass-detector).
